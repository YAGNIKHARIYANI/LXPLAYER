LX-PLAYER MOCKUP PRESENTATION

Designed by Brother & Sister Team for the Web Development Competition

Collapsible Sidebar Drawer

The menu hides on mobile to reclaim 64px width. A tap on the top-left menu icon slides it open over the screen with labels always visible.

Symmetric 2-Column Grid

Cards display in two clean columns with a balanced 12px gap. A 32px offset keeps movie posters from touching the sidebar boundary.

Private Streaming Player

Taps open a private streaming modal centered vertically on mobile. It is rounded fully as a premium card, removing any awkward top spacing gaps.

Sleeker Indicator Dots

Carousel indicators are converted to tiny centered dots (4px-6px) and positioned below the banner image, resolving clashing.

Non-Wrapping Pages

Double-arrow buttons are hidden on mobile screens, ensuring the numbers fit comfortably in a single line without wrapping.
